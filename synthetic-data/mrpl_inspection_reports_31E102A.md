# Inspection Reports – 31-E-102A (Feed/Effluent Heat Exchanger), DHDT Unit-31

**Company:** Mangalore Refinery and Petrochemicals Limited (MRPL) – synthetic demonstration data
**Reports included:** IR-2019-03, IR-2023-11, IR-2025-04, IR-2025-06 (chronological)
**Prepared by:** Inspection Department (Static Equipment)

> **SYNTHETIC / DUMMY DATA.** Not an actual MRPL record. Detailed thickness readings are in `mrpl_inspection_readings_31E102A.xlsx`.

Acceptance basis: minimum tube wall 1.90 mm; tube plugging limit 10 % (64 of 640 tubes); plug tubes with wall loss above 70 % or with a confirmed leak.

---

## 1. IR-2019-03 – Turnaround Inspection (18 March 2019)

**Inspector:** V. Shenoy (Static Inspection) **Scope:** full bundle pull, hydrojetting, 100 % eddy current testing (ECT), hydrotest.

- Shell-side fouling moderate; removed by hydrojetting. No pitting on tubesheets.
- ECT: 628 tubes below 20 % wall loss, 12 tubes in the 20–39 % range. No tubes plugged.
- Minimum tube wall 2.45 mm (row 1, inlet end).
- Pass partition gasket replaced as a routine item.
- Hydrotest at 1.25× design: no leaks.
- **Conclusion:** fit for four more years of service. Next inspection at the next planned turnaround or opportunity.

## 2. IR-2023-11 – Opportunity Shutdown Inspection (9 November 2023)

**Inspector:** V. Shenoy **Scope:** partial pull (channel and floating-head covers removed), 100 % ECT, visual examination, hydrotest.

- ECT: 561 tubes below 20 %, 47 tubes 20–39 %, 23 tubes 40–59 %, 9 tubes 60–79 %. No tube above 80 %.
- **4 tubes plugged** (wall loss above 70 %). Total plugged: 4 (0.6 %).
- Minimum tube wall 2.31 mm (row 1, inlet end). Wall loss concentrated in rows 1–4 near the tube inlet.
- Light under-deposit corrosion observed on outside surface of tubes in rows 1–3.
- Pass partition gasket surface acceptable; new gasket fitted.
- Hydrotest: no leaks.
- **Recommendation:** monitor rows 1–4 at each shutdown; consider ordering a spare bundle if wall loss rate rises.

## 3. IR-2025-04 – Planned Shutdown Inspection (14 April 2025)

**Inspector:** A. Prabhu (Static Inspection) **Scope:** channel cover removal, 100 % ECT, thickness readings, visual, hydrotest, gasket inspection.

- ECT as-found: 4 previously plugged; 498 tubes below 20 %; 71 tubes 20–39 %; 40 tubes 40–59 %; 20 tubes 60–79 %; 7 tubes at or above 80 %.
- **7 tubes plugged** (those at or above 80 %). Total plugged: 11 (1.7 %).
- **27 tubes above 60 % wall loss** (20 + 7), of which 20 remain in service.
- Minimum tube wall 2.14 mm (row 1); margin over the 1.90 mm minimum is 0.24 mm.
- Wall loss rate for row 1 increased to about 0.12 mm/year (0.03 mm/year 2019–2023).
- Pass partition gasket weeping observed; gasket replaced.
- Under-deposit corrosion deeper in rows 1–4; light pitting (max depth 0.4 mm) on tubesheet face near the inlet.
- Hydrotest: no leaks after plugging and gasket replacement.
- **Recommendation:** replace the tube bundle at the next turnaround (February 2027). Order a spare bundle; interim monitoring by ΔP trending and quarterly ECT screening of rows 1–6.
- **Status of recommendation at the time of the June 2025 failure:** no purchase requisition raised; owner and due date not assigned.

## 4. IR-2025-06 – Emergency Inspection after Product Failure (16 June 2025)

**Inspector:** A. Prabhu **Scope:** channel cover removal, shell-side hydrotest at 1.25× design pressure, targeted ECT on rows 1–6, full re-classification of the bundle, visual.

- **14 tubes found leaking** at the tube ends and tubesheet (water weeping under shell-side test). All in rows 1–4 near the inlet; tube coordinates recorded in the report.
- ECT as-found classification: 11 previously plugged; 471 tubes below 20 %; 78 tubes 20–39 %; 44 tubes 40–59 %; 24 tubes 60–79 %; 12 tubes at or above 80 %.
- **14 tubes plugged** (12 at or above 80 % plus 2 leaking tubes in the 60–79 % bin). Total plugged: 25 (3.9 %).
- Minimum tube wall 2.08 mm (row 1), 0.06 mm lower than in April 2025; margin over the 1.90 mm minimum is now 0.18 mm.
- Tubesheet pitting max depth 0.5 mm near the inlet rows (0.4 mm in April 2025).
- Pass partition gasket renewed again; bolt torque recorded.
- Hydrotest after plugging: no leaks.
- **Conclusion:** exchanger fit to return to service for a limited period. Remaining life for row 1 tubes estimated at less than 12 months at the current wall loss rate.
- **Recommendation:** install a replacement bundle at the earliest opportunity (Tender 0412 for the new bundle was floated on 12 July 2025); continue quarterly ECT screening; keep 31-E-102A under increased monitoring until replacement.

## 7. Trend Statement

Between 2019 and June 2025 wall loss in the inlet rows accelerated sharply. The plugged-tube count went 0 → 4 → 11 → 25. The wall loss rate is not linear: the 2023–2025 rate was about four times the 2019–2023 rate. The April 2025 report flagged the risk one month before the first off-spec reading in May 2025.
