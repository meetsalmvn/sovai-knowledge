## 2. Summary – "What Happened Last Time"

The most recent significant event on 31-E-102A was the **emergency shutdown and repair of 14–20 June 2025**. Treated diesel from DHDT Unit-31 had failed its sulphur specification three times since 14 May 2025. On 14 June the unit was shut down. A shell-side hydrotest found **14 leaking tubes** in the inlet rows. The tubes were plugged, raising the total plugged tubes from 11 to **25 of 640 (3.9 %)**, the channel gasket was renewed, and the unit restarted on 20 June 2025. Product sulphur returned to 6–8 ppm. Root cause was under-deposit corrosion causing wall thinning of the inlet tube rows. A replacement bundle was ordered under Tender MRPL/PUR/MECH/2025-26/0412 (floated 12 July 2025) and delivered on 28 May 2026. The bundle has not yet been installed.

## 4. Recurring Observations

1. Inlet-row tubes (rows 1–4) show the fastest wall loss in all inspections since 2023.
2. Gasket weeping at the channel and pass partition gasket has occurred in 2021, 2024 and April 2025.
3. Fouling on the shell side was moderate in 2017 and lighter after hydrojetting in 2019; fouling was **not** the driver of the 2025 failure.
4. Plugged tube count has increased at each inspection since 2023 (0 → 4 → 11 → 25).
